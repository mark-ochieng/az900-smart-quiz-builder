## Slide 2: The problem (20%)
- Learners practise with questions but don't understand why an answer is right or wrong.
- They memorise answers, then fail when the wording changes.
- No quick way to see which topics are weak.

**Say:** Add one line from your own AZ-900 preparation: a moment you got an answer right by guessing, or wrong without knowing why.

---

## Slide 3: The solution and demo (25%)
- Instant feedback and a short explanation after every answer
- 48 original questions across 3 AZ-900 topics
- Retry only missed questions
- Readiness tracker by topic
- Mobile-first, light and dark mode

**Demo:** Screen-record 30 to 45 seconds: pick a topic, answer one question wrong, show the explanation, finish, show the results and the retry button. Paste the video into this slide.

---

## Slide 4: Target users (15%)
- Beginners preparing for AZ-900, especially students and career changers
- Learners on phones with limited data, since it is a lightweight single page
- Study groups and cohorts that want a shared practice resource

**How they use it:** 10-minute daily rounds, then retry mistakes.

---

## Slide 5: Impact and scalability (20%)
- Question bank is a simple array: swap it for AI-900, AZ-104 or other certifications
- Add Supabase for accounts, leaderboards and cohort analytics
- Add local-language explanations and African examples
- Community-contributed questions with review

---

## Slide 6: How it was built (prompt engineering and speed)
- Built with Claude using structured prompts: role, goal, constraints, format
- Original questions only, with plausible distractors and explanations
- Fast iteration: working app, then upgrade with retry mode and readiness tracker
- Full prompts in the GitHub repo (PROMPT_LOG.md)
