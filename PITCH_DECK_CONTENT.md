## Slide 3: Problem / Idea
**Heading:** Learners practise, but don't understand

- Many AZ-900 learners practise with questions but can't explain why an answer is right or wrong.
- They memorise answers, then get stuck when the wording changes.
- They don't know which topics are weak until it is too late.
- [Add one sentence from your own AZ-900 preparation: a time you guessed right or got it wrong without knowing why.]

---

## Slide 4: Solution
**Heading:** A quiz that teaches

- Instant feedback after every answer
- A clear explanation for every question, right or wrong
- Questions organised by the three AZ-900 topics
- Retry only the questions you missed
- A readiness tracker that shows accuracy by topic
- Works on any phone, no sign-up, no install

---

## Slide 5: Product
**Heading:** How it works

1. Pick a topic and a quiz length (5, 10 or 20 questions).
2. Answer a question and see right or wrong straight away.
3. Read the short explanation.
4. Finish and see your score by topic.
5. Retry only your mistakes and watch your readiness grow.

## Slide 9: Competitive Advantage
**Heading:** Why Smart Quiz Builder wins

- Explanation for every answer, not only the score
- Retry-missed mode targets weak spots
- Readiness tracker by topic
